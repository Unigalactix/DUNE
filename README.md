# DUNE — The Complete Saga Explained

A professional, interactive infographic website explaining the entire Dune saga in plain English — based on Frank Herbert's books and the films/series.

**Live design reference:** modelled on the structure of [JINX](https://unigalactix.github.io/JINX/) — dark cinematic theme, sticky header, numbered sections, stat blocks, cards and interactive selectors — with a Dune palette of deep black-brown, sand (#d6b25e / #c19a49), spice orange and bone text. Display font: Cinzel; body: Inter/system.

## What's inside

- **Hero** — "Fear is the mind-killer", key stats (6 novels, 10,191 AG, 1 spice planet)
- **01 — Dune in 60 Seconds** — spice-flow diagram: sandworms → spice → Guild Navigators → all space travel depends on Arrakis
- **02 — The Story Explained** — the novel in 6 clear acts, endings spoiler-gated
- **03 — Screen Saga** — clickable breakdowns for:
  - Dune (1984) — David Lynch
  - Frank Herbert's Dune (2000) — miniseries
  - Frank Herbert's Children of Dune (2003)
  - Dune: Part One (2021) — Denis Villeneuve
  - Dune: Part Two (2024) — Villeneuve
  - Dune: Prophecy (2024– ) — HBO prequel, Bene Gesserit origins
  - Dune: Part Three — **UPCOMING, scheduled Dec 18, 2026**, adapting *Dune Messiah*
  - Bonus: Jodorowsky's Dune (unmade)
- **04 — Interactive Timeline** — 12 clickable events from the Prophecy era through *Chapterhouse: Dune*, including God Emperor's 3,500-year reign
- **05 — Characters** — 18 searchable, faction-filterable cards with Wikipedia/Wikimedia actor portraits (Villeneuve cast)
- **06 — Houses & Factions** — Atreides, Harkonnen, Corrino/Imperium, Fremen, Bene Gesserit, Spacing Guild, Mentats, CHOAM
- **07 — World of Arrakis + Interactive Spice Cycle** — 5 clickable steps, plus stillsuit, crysknife, ornithopter, Shai-Hulud, Sardaukar
- **08 — The Books** — Herbert's 6 novels in reading order + note on Brian Herbert & Kevin J. Anderson's expanded books
- **09 — Glossary** — 16 searchable terms in plain English
- **10 — Themes, Litany Against Fear, Part Two ending & Part Three preview** (spoiler-gated)

## Interactivity (vanilla JS, no build step)

Screen-saga selector, timeline selector, spice-cycle selector, character search + faction filter, glossary search, spoiler toggle (spoilers blurred until enabled), smooth-scroll nav, mobile menu.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then visit http://localhost:8000
```
